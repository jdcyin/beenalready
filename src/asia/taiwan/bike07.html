Day 7, 8: Doulan 都蘭 > Zhiben 知本

Stay: Zhiben Hot Spring Hotel

‍

I've been on the road for a week. I get in contact with my dad and stepmom to update him on my travel. They decide to be spontaneous and say that they want to meet me in a town in Taitung County called Zhiben known for its hot springs. I decide to take two days out to spend it with them. I was just so glad to be in a hot spring town. A lot of hot spring baths and just chilling, and nice food for once. After having been on the road for a week I appreciated it so much more.
