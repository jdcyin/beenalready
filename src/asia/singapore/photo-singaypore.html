Why "SIN.GAY.PORE"? It's a jab at "homosexuality being a sin" according to the penal code in Singapore, but also visibility of the gay scene here, despite conceptualizations (domestic, or international) of rigidity and oppression of the local government. So here's "Singaypore", a small photo diary into celebrating gender, expression, and general debauchery amongst locals and foreigners -- mostly gay men, but not exclusively so -- who have clustered on the Little Red Dot.
