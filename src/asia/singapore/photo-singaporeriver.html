SINGAPORE RIVER

2017-03-23, 10:16, JASON

‍

February 11, 2017. ‍Thorin and I live a 15 minute walk to a small inlet of the Singapore River. Here are pictures taken on the 15th day, and formally last day, of Chinese New Year celebrations. There is a breeze brought in by the stagnant water to our left, and the distant noise of percussion drums and cymbals, indicating a lion dance nearby.
