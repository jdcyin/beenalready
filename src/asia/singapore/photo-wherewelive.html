WHERE WE LIVE

2017-03-15, 14:39, JASON

‍We've lived in Singapore for over two years now. I pass by these places nearly everyday, but I wanted to capture them at different moments so I'll always remember days in this house, and the surrounding neighborhood. Two years ago, I couldn't locate Singapore on a map. Now, it's home.
