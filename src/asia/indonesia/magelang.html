MAGELANG, INDONESIA

Sites: Borobudur Temple

2026-01-19

I've been waiting 10 years to come to Borobudur.

It was a bit more underwhelming than I thought, but still spectacular. Centuries of histories carved onto the bas reliefs, telling the stories of incest, sex, gambling, enlightenment and other things of human nature and spirituality. Beautiful.
