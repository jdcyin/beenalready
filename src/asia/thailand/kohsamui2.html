KOH SAMUI, THAILAND

2024-07-23

STAY: Lek Lek Villa

Roshan is a friend from University of California, Irvine. He grew up in Hong Kong but studied in the U.S. Christy, his wife, is Singaporean and Hongkongese. They decided on a destination wedding in Koh Samui, marking my second trip there. My thoughts are still the same from before. For tourism in Thai islands, I think Koh Samui is a solid skip. Too overrun with resorts and backpacker style tourism.

The wedding, however, was lovely. There were wedding welcome drinks at a small beachside bar, with the ceremony at a separate villa on top of the mountains. And, the recovery day was also at the same villa. I had too much fun and was not taking pictures, which explains the no picture post and short post.

I will unlikely be back to Koh Samui unless occasion calls for it.
